# MyEditor v4.0

## Highlights

- **A real writing app** - menus, a toolbar and a format bar over selected words for headings, lists, checklists, tables, footnotes, code and links, with each system's own keys. Type `/` to insert anything.
- **Your folders, beside your writing** - add folders to the sidebar and open what is in them: notes, pictures, films, PDFs and office documents, with Quick Look on Space. Rename or move a file, and the links in your notes follow.
- **Photo groups** - pictures that follow each other become a calm mosaic in which each keeps its shape, in the editor, the preview, HTML, PDF and print.
- **Spreadsheets** - Excel, OpenDocument and CSV files open as tables you can edit, with formulas in your language, and saving keeps the rest of the file as it was.
- **Lock a note with a password** - it is encrypted in place, in the open age format, so it opens without MyEditor too.
- **MyEditor in German** - View > Language switches the whole app.
- **Dictate and listen** - dictate with your system's dictation, or offline on this computer after a one-time download; Edit > Speech reads a draft aloud.

New to MyEditor? [Getting started](https://github.com/rinbal/my_editor/blob/main/docs/use/getting-started.md) takes ten minutes, and the [user guide](https://github.com/rinbal/my_editor/blob/main/docs/README.md) has the details.

## Writing

- **Spelling as you type** - your system's own spell checker, with suggestions on right-click.
- **Write, Preview and Split** - your text as written, as it reads, or both side by side.
- **A readable column** - text keeps to a comfortable line length in a wide window, while code runs full width.
- **Your font and size** - choose them in Settings, and zoom with View > Zoom In and Zoom Out.
- **Faster formatting** - Markdown Shortcuts format as you type, and Undo takes back whole runs of typing.
- **Audio and video in a note** - they show as a card with their first frame and play in the app; HTML exports play them too.
- **Keyboard first** - Ctrl+Tab and F6 move between tabs and panels, and Help lists every shortcut. Controls are named for screen readers, and focus is always visible.

## Your work, kept safe

- **Calm notices** - when a file changes on the disk, moves or is deleted, a quiet notice says so and offers what to do. Saving over a newer version asks first.
- **Updates that wait for you** - a new version shows as an **Update Available** button instead of an interruption. Software Update installs in place on every system, checks every download and brings back every tab.
- **Pictures that behave** - a phone's portrait photo stands upright, pictures never run past the page, and a paragraph that runs over a page in a PDF or a print no longer repeats a line on the next.

## Nostr, if you use it

All of this stays out of sight until you use a Nostr account.

- **Accounts without a signer app** - create, restore and back up an account in the app, move its key to a signer app later, and edit your profile.
- **Article Details** - title, summary, cover and hashtags in one place and at the top of the page, with a 16:9 cover crop.
- **Mentions, quotes and co-authors** - type `@` to mention someone, quote notes and articles, credit co-authors and share an article's zaps with them, as STANDUP does.
- **Media that waits for you** - pictures, audio and video stay on this computer until you publish, and the text shows which ones. Settings can upload them right away instead.
- **Media Library, rebuilt** - drag files in, filter and search, and Quick Look plays audio and video. Uploads to the default media servers work, and the progress says what it waits for.
- **Drafts and Published, redesigned** - drafts grouped by date with a full preview on Space, and your posts open on the web in the reader you choose.
- **Imports bring everything** - the whole text, its pictures and details from every source, in one Imports window that checks your feeds while the app runs.
- **Night Violet** - the Nostr parts of the app share one calm violet look, in light and dark.
- **EINUNDZWANZIG membership** - apply and pay by Lightning from the Nostr menu, and see your membership, relay, storage and Nostr address at a glance.

## Technical updates & fixes

- New dependencies: `PyYAML`, `pyrage`, `rubicon-objc` on macOS, and `sherpa-onnx` with `sherpa-onnx-core` on Windows and Linux; newly bundled QtMultimedia and QtTextToSpeech. The .deb depends on `libpulse0` and recommends `libenchant-2-2`; read aloud on Linux uses `speech-dispatcher`.
- Dictation models and the R Markdown tools download from their makers, each file pinned to its size and SHA-256. Builds install from hashed, wheels-only lock files, every bundle runs `my-editor --self-test` before it becomes an installer, and CI runs the full suite on all three systems.
- Security: a secret key is never pasted into text, published or sent to a search relay, and a copied one leaves the clipboard after a minute; relays others name may not point into the private network; public copies of pictures lose their hidden data; an imported feed can no longer make the app open an address or a file; the membership service keeps the association's API key on its own server (`sidecar/`).
- Help > Show Log Files opens a small log for bug reports, never with keys or text in it.
- Submitting an article to a STANDUP publication's editorial team is built in and stays hidden until the association names its submission server ([how to try it](https://github.com/rinbal/my_editor/blob/main/docs/develop/standup-submissions.md)).
- Fixes: the Style and Color menus could vanish after a right-click; line numbers sat one line above their line; HTML documents reopened in monospace; emoji shifted mentions, selections and Find results; closed tabs now free their memory; locking a document showed it as changed outside MyEditor; on Linux, a change made outside after a save went unnoticed; Markdown with soft line breaks opens properly; a wide picture in a PDF made on a Mac stays within the page; relays could replace one article with another (the `d` tag now comes first).
