# EINUNDZWANZIG membership

EINUNDZWANZIG is a Bitcoin association whose members get Nostr services of their own. **Nostr > EINUNDZWANZIG Membership…** lets you apply, pay the yearly fee with Lightning, and set up what membership brings, without leaving MyEditor.

## Become a member

1. Choose **Nostr > EINUNDZWANZIG Membership…** with your Nostr account in use. If none is in use, the window offers **Connect Signer App…**.
2. **Become an EINUNDZWANZIG Member** shows what membership includes and the yearly fee, in sats first, then in Swiss francs and euros. The amount the association sets is exact; the others are converted with today's Bitcoin price and say "about". The invoice's sats are always exact.
3. Fill in the application, read the statutes (**Open Statutes**), and pay the Lightning invoice: scan it with your wallet, **Copy Invoice**, or **Pay in Browser**. **I’ve Paid** checks for your payment.

Membership runs for a calendar year, until December 31. You renew it yourself; nothing is charged automatically.

## What membership brings

The member page shows each service as a row, as System Settings shows iCloud+. A row MyEditor can set up has one button at its end until it is done, then a check mark and a status:

- **Members' relay**: added to your relay list, so your posts reach it.
- **Nostr address**: **Choose…** a name, and MyEditor offers to show it on your profile (**Add to Profile**). **Not Now** keeps the button there for later.
- **Media storage**: 5 GB on the members' media server, used by your [Media Library](media.md). The row says how much is used and opens the library.
- **STANDUP**: the association's publishing platform. There is nothing to set up: **Open Submissions** shows what you [handed in to its publications](publish.md#submit-to-a-publication).

Services you set up on the association's website, such as the watchtower and the community group, have a row with **Open Guide**.

**Manage** holds the receipt, the membership details, changing or copying your Nostr address, and exporting or deleting your membership data.

## When something is not available

When the membership service cannot be reached, the member page says which parts are unavailable for now, with **Try Again**. If joining from the app is not possible at all, the window offers **Join on the Website** instead. Members get their relay and media server either way.
