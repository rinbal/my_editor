# Media Library

The Media Library keeps your pictures, films, sound and other files on media servers you choose, and lets you put them into any note or article. It works like a Finder window.

## Open it

- **Nostr > Media Library…** (**⇧⌘M** / **Ctrl+Shift+M**) opens the library.
- **Nostr > Insert from Media Library…** (**⇧⌘I** / **Ctrl+Shift+I**), or **Insert > From Media Library…**, opens it to pick a file for the document. So does **Choose…** at an article's cover.

## Find your files

The toolbar has **Add…**, the kind of file to show (all, pictures, films, sound or other files), the order (newest, oldest, largest, smallest, name), a search field for names, and an action menu (**⋯**) with the commands for the selected files, **Refresh** and **Media Servers…**.

Files show as square tiles with their name and the day they were added. Films show a frame and PDFs their first page.

## Use the keys you know

As in the Finder: the arrow keys move, **Space** shows a file in Quick Look (films and sound play there), **Return** or a double-click inserts it when you are picking, **⌘C** / **Ctrl+C** copies the links, **⌘V** / **Ctrl+V** adds a pasted picture, and **Delete** deletes, after asking. The right-click menu and the action menu have **Insert**, **Quick Look**, **Copy Link**, **Open in Browser**, **Save As…** (**Save to Folder…** for several files), **Stop Sharing** and **Delete**.

## Add files

Click **Add…**, drag files onto the library, or paste a picture. Each new file shows as a tile with a ring that fills as it uploads; the **×** in the ring cancels. A file that does not upload says why under it, with **Retry**. Closing the window does not stop an upload.

Drag a tile onto a document to put it there.

## Who can see a file

Anything you add can be opened by anyone who has its link, as the line at the foot of the library says. That is how media servers work.

Some other Nostr apps keep files privately, encrypted. Those carry a small lock in the corner ("Only you can see this"). Putting a private picture into a document asks first, then makes a separate public copy without camera and location details. **Stop Sharing** deletes that public copy again; the private original stays.

## Media servers

**Media Servers…** in the action menu lists your servers in the order uploads use them: new files go to the first, and the others keep copies. **Make Primary**, **Remove** and **Add Server** change the list. Servers your Nostr account already lists are offered there. **Share with Other Apps…** publishes your list, so other Nostr apps find your files.

Until you choose your own, MyEditor uses two independent servers, nostr.download and blossom.primal.net. EINUNDZWANZIG members also get the members' server, with 5 GB of their own; the line at the foot of the library then says how much is used.
