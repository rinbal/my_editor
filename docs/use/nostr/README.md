# Publishing to Nostr

MyEditor is a writing app first. If you also want to publish what you write, it can send your notes and articles to [Nostr](https://nostr.com), an open network where no company owns your account or your readers. This part is optional: until you choose it, MyEditor shows nothing of Nostr except the **Nostr** menu.

## What you get

- **Publish as a note or an article.** A short note for a quick thought, a long-form article with a title, a summary, a cover and hashtags. [Publish](publish.md)
- **Submit to a publication.** Hand a text in to a publication on EINUNDZWANZIG STANDUP and talk it through with its editorial team. [Submit](publish.md#submit-to-a-publication)
- **Private drafts on every device.** Drafts are encrypted so that only you can read them, and they follow you to every computer and phone where you use the same account. [Drafts](drafts.md)
- **A media library.** Your pictures, films and sound on media servers you choose, ready to put into any article. [Media Library](media.md)
- **Imports.** Bring your blog or a website you follow into drafts, from a feed, an export file or a single link. [Imports](imports.md)
- **EINUNDZWANZIG membership.** Join the EINUNDZWANZIG Bitcoin association from inside the app, and get its relay, a Nostr address and media storage. [Membership](einundzwanzig.md)

## Start

Open the **Nostr** menu and choose one way in:

- **Create Account…** if you are new to Nostr. It takes a minute, and helps you save a backup.
- **Connect Signer App…** if you already use a signer app such as Amber, Primal, Amethyst or Bunker46. Your key stays in that app.
- **Restore Account…** if you have a backup file or your private key.

[Your account](account.md) walks through each one. Once an account is in use, its picture sits at the right end of the tab row, and the Nostr features appear where they help: in the Insert menu, the slash menu and the Drafts panel. **Nostr > Sign Out** takes them away again.

## Words you will meet

- **Relay**: a server that passes your posts on to readers. MyEditor picks good ones for you.
- **Signer app**: an app that holds your private key and approves each post, so MyEditor never sees the key.
- **Media server**: a server that keeps your pictures and other files for your posts.
- **Zap**: a small Bitcoin payment a reader sends to say thanks.

How MyEditor talks to the network, for the curious and for developers, is in [How MyEditor speaks Nostr](../../develop/nostr.md).
