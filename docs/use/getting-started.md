# Getting started

In about ten minutes you write a first note with a heading, a checklist and a few pictures, save it in a folder of your own, and open a PDF. You need no account for any of it.

Keys are given for a Mac first, then for Windows and Linux: **⌘S** / **Ctrl+S**.

## 1. Install and open MyEditor

Download MyEditor for your computer from the [install guide](https://rinbal.github.io/my_editor/install/). It walks you through the security question your system asks the first time. The same steps as text are in [Install MyEditor](install.md).

The first time it opens, MyEditor shows a **Welcome** tab with the few keys worth knowing. It is a note like any other: read it, then close it. You can open it again from **Help > Welcome**.

## 2. Write a note

1. Choose **File > New** (**⌘N** / **Ctrl+N**). A new tab opens.
2. Type a title, for example *Two days at the mountain lake*.
3. Make it a heading: choose **Format > Style > Heading 1** (**⌥⌘1** / **Ctrl+1**), or pick it in the style pop-up on the toolbar.
4. Press **Return** and write a sentence or two. A new line after a heading is ordinary text again.
5. Type **What to pack next time**, make it **Heading 2**, press **Return**, and choose **Format > Checklist** (**⇧⌘9** / **Ctrl+Shift+9**). Write a few items, one per line. Click a box to tick it.

> [!TIP]
> Type `/` at the start of a word for a short list of everything you can insert: headings, lists, a table, a picture. Type a few letters to narrow it and press **Return**.

Select a few words with the pointer and a small bar appears over them, with Bold, Italic, a link and the paragraph style. Everything else is in the **Format** and **Insert** menus.

## 3. Add pictures

Choose **Insert > Image…** and pick a picture, or drag one from the Finder or Explorer onto the page.

Add two more right below it. Pictures that follow each other, with nothing but empty lines between them, become a **photo group**: a calm mosaic in which every picture keeps its shape. Point at a picture to see its small buttons: **ALT** for a description that screen readers read aloud, **Large** to give it a row of its own, and **More** for everything else. [Pictures and photo groups](pictures.md) has the details.

## 4. Save it

Choose **File > Save** (**⌘S** / **Ctrl+S**), give the note a name and choose where it goes. Notes are saved as Markdown (`.md`), plain text that every other app can open.

Pictures you added are copied into a folder beside the note, named after it (`Mountain lake_media`), so the note and its pictures stay together wherever you move them.

From now on **⌘S** / **Ctrl+S** saves without asking. What you type is also [backed up as you go](privacy.md#backups-while-you-type), so a crash never costs you your words.

## 5. Keep your notes in a folder

The sidebar on the left shows the folders you choose.

1. Choose **File > Add Folder to Sidebar…** (**⌃⌘T** on a Mac), or click **Add Folder…** in the sidebar, and pick the folder your note is in.
2. Click the folder. The file list beside it shows its files, newest first.
3. Select a file and press **Space** to look at it without opening it. Press **Return** to open it in a tab.

Everything you do here happens to the real files: there is no hidden library. If you rename a picture or move a note, the links in your other notes follow it. See [Folders and files](folders-and-files.md).

## 6. Open a PDF

Open any PDF with **File > Open…** (**⌘O** / **Ctrl+O**), or click one in the file list. It opens in a quiet reader: **Space** goes down a screen, **n** and **p** go to the next and previous page, and **F12** shows its contents. MyEditor remembers the page you were on. See [Reading PDFs](pdf.md).

## What next

- Make the window yours: **View > Appearance** for light or dark, **View > Paper Mode** for a page of its own, and **Settings…** (**⌘,** / **Ctrl+,**) for the font and its size.
- [Writing](writing.md): lists, tables, code, links, find and replace, spelling and dictation.
- [Save, export and print](save-export-print.md): HTML, PDF, Word-friendly formats and printing.
- [Spreadsheets](spreadsheets.md) and [Privacy and safety](privacy.md), including locking a note with a password.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md), or **Help > Keyboard Shortcuts** in the app.
- Want to publish what you write? [Publishing to Nostr](nostr/README.md) is optional and stays out of the way until you choose it.
